about

First-year CS student at UC Berkeley (Class of 2030). I build AI systems for problems that actually matter: smart glasses that help visually impaired users navigate independently, a drone network that brings healthcare access to rural areas, and models that turn messy medical data into something a doctor can act on.

I came to this work personally. After experiencing vision challenges myself, assistive tech stopped being an abstract research interest and started being the point. A few proof points: 1st place at an AI hackathon for an NLP speech coach, 5,000+ students taught to code through Kids Teach Tech, and a community campaign that reached 20,000+ people. I move fast, ship working systems (not just notebooks), and I'm looking for research, internship, or build opportunities where getting the technical details right changes someone's actual life.
